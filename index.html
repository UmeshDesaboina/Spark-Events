Good to Know

How early should I book?

We suggest booking two to three weeks ahead, and earlier for weekends, festival dates and wedding season.

Do you decorate at home as well as in halls?

Yes. We decorate homes, banquet halls, farmhouses and party venues across Hyderabad.

Can I get a custom theme or character?

Yes. Share your idea, colours or a reference photo and we'll design a 2D or 3D theme around it.

Can I add car entry, fog entry or a candy counter?

Yes. Add any of these to a package. Select them in the booking form and we'll include them in your quote.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your details on WhatsApp, finalise the theme and quote with our team, and confirm your date with an advance.
